New Recording 28

The meeting focused on finalizing the logistics and curriculum for an upcoming three-day AI research program. The group prioritized streamlining technical onboarding through local scripts and refined the instructional flow to emphasize ethics and practical research applications.

Strategic Overview
The program aims to bring participants up to speed on agentic AI, transitioning from theoretical understanding to hands-on research applications. A critical decision was made to shift from centralized "Team" subscriptions to a "Bring Your Own Subscription" model to bypass contractual and legal friction, with costs reimbursed via a dedicated cost center.

Curriculum & Instructional Design

Program Schedule & Curriculum

• Day 1: Foundational Skills - Project organization, technical setup, and a mandatory Ethics & Privacy module (scraping, NDAs, and data leak prevention).
• Day 2: Research Ecosystems - Literature search automation using university database APIs and $100 in free API credits.
• Day 3: Replication & Modeling - Replication of econometric models and theory research. Discussion on AI's comparative advantage in solving complex derivations.

• Instructional Shift: Ethics, safety, and privacy modules were moved to Day 1 to ensure participants are grounded in responsible AI use before beginning exercises.
• Methodology: Focus on "reviving old research dreams"—using AI to solve complex derivations (e.g., fifth-order polynomials) that were previously computationally prohibitive.
• Tooling Decision: The group decided to deprioritize "Lint" (mathematical verification software) to avoid distracting from the core agentic AI curriculum and to maintain focus on human-understandable intuition in research.

Technical Setup & Logistics
• Deployment: Georgie has developed PowerShell scripts for Windows and is finalizing a Mac script. Manual instructions will be provided for outliers (~20% expected failure rate due to University-managed machine restrictions).
• Platform: Participants must register for CloudCode; one month of "Pro" access will be provided.
• Location: Room 402.
• Timing: Sessions run from 18:00 to 21:00, with food available from 17:30.

Key Decisions & Friction
• Troubleshooting Strategy: To avoid stalling lectures, participants struggling with setup will be paired with tech-proficient peers for real-time assistance.
• Journal Requirements: Discussion highlighted that AI-generated proofs must be translated into human-understandable intuition to be viable for journal publication; pure computer verification is insufficient for peer acceptance.
